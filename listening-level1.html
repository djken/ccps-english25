Listening and Reading - Level 1

Part One

1 -Greeting

Hello! My name is David. Every morning, I greet my teachers and classmates. I say “Good morning” and smile. When I see my friends, I say “Hi” or “Hey.” Greeting people is important because it shows respect and kindness.

2 - Self-introduction

Hi there! My name is Maria. I am 17 years old and I am from Cap-Haïtien, Haiti. I live with my parents and my younger brother. I am a student at Central High School.
I like reading books, listening to music, and playing volleyball with my friends. In the mornings, I usually wake up early, have breakfast, and go to school. I like meeting new people and making friends. Nice to meet you!

3 - Personal Information

Good afternoon! My name is Daniel. I am fourteen years old. I live in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, with my parents and my older sister. My father is a teacher, and my mother is a nurse. I have one dog named Lucky. I go to Central School, and my favorite subject is English. In my free time, I like reading books and playing football with my friends. I love my family very much, and we often spend time together on the weekends.

4 - Daily Routines

Hello! My name is Sophie. I am thirteen years old and I live in Jacmel, Haiti. I usually wake up at six o’clock in the morning. First, I brush my teeth and wash my face. Then, I eat breakfast with my family. After breakfast, I go to school by bus.

I study English, math, and science at school. In the afternoon, I come home and do my homework. Sometimes, I play football with my friends or listen to music. In the evening, I eat dinner with my family and then read a book before going to bed at nine o’clock.

5 - Places and Directions

Hello! My name is Laura. I am 19 years old and I live in Cap-Haitian, Haiti. Every day, I go to school in the morning. My school is near my house, so I usually walk. After school, I sometimes go to the market to buy fruits and vegetables. My favorite place is the library because I like reading books there. When I want to go to the bank, I go straight from my house and then turn right. The post office is next to the bank. I like exploring my city and visiting different places with my friends.

6 - Verbs of movement

Hello! My name is Alex. Every morning, I walk to school with my friends. After school, I run in the park and jump with my little brother. In the evening, I go home and sit with my family. On weekends, we travel to visit our grandparents. I like moving and staying active every day.

7 - Numbers, Colors & Clothes

Hello! My name is Alex. I am fourteen years old and I live in Port-au-Prince, Haiti. I have many clothes. I have three red shirts, two blue pants, and one green hat. I like wearing my red shirt and blue pants to school. I also have four pairs of shoes: one black pair, one brown pair, and two white pairs. My favorite color is blue, and I like wearing blue clothes on the weekends. Sometimes, I help my little sister choose her clothes too. I enjoy organizing my clothes and keeping them clean.

8 - Size and Measurement

Hello! My name is Tom. I have a big school bag. The bag is heavy because I have many books. My pencil is short, and my ruler is long. My school is very big, and my classroom is small. I use size words every day to describe things around me.

9 - Shopping & Money
Every Saturday, I go to the market with my mother. We buy rice, beans, and vegetables. Today, I also buy a new shirt for school. The shirt costs two hundred gourdes. I give the money to the seller and he gives me the shirt. After shopping, we drink water and talk with friends at the market. I like helping my mother and learning how to buy things. Shopping is fun when you know the price and you pay with the right money.
10 - Weather & Seasons
Good evening! My name is Claire. I am 25 years old and I live in Jacmel, Haiti. Today, it is sunny and warm. I like sunny days because I can go outside and play with my friends. In winter, it is usually cooler and sometimes windy. My favorite season is summer because I can go to the beach and swim in the sea. I also like spring because the flowers are beautiful and the weather is nice. I always wear light clothes when it is hot and warm clothes when it is cold. Weather and seasons are very important because they change what we do every day.

Part Two

11 - Hobbies & Free Time
Hello! My name is Paul. I am 36 years old and I live in Port-au-Prince, Haiti. In my free time, I like reading books and listening to music. I also enjoy playing football with my friends after school. On weekends, I sometimes go to the park or watch movies with my family. My favorite hobby is drawing because I can use my imagination and create new things. I also like singing songs at home. Free time is very important because it helps me relax and have fun.
12 - Health & Body

Hello! My name is Sophie. I am fourteen years old and I live in Cap-Haïtien, Haiti. Sometimes, I feel sick, so I need to rest at home. Yesterday, I had a headache and a stomachache. I went to bed early and drank warm water. I have a younger brother who is healthy and plays outside every day. I take care of my body by eating fruits and vegetables, drinking water, and exercising. When I feel tired, I take a short rest. It is important to stay healthy and take care of our body every day.

13 - Feelings & Emotions

Hello! My name is Anna. Today, I feel happy because I went to the park with my friends. My friend Paul feels excited because he is going to a football match. My little brother feels tired because he played all morning. It is important to understand our feelings and talk about them with friends and family.

14 - Transportation & Travel

Hello! My name is Daniel. I am 40 years old and I live in Port-au-Prince, Haiti. Every day, I go to school by bus. The bus stops near my house, and I take it in the morning. Sometimes, I go to the market with my mother by car. On Sundays, my family and I travel to the beach by car. I like traveling because I can see new places and spend time with my family. When I want to go to the bank or the post office, I usually walk. I enjoy exploring the city and learning the names of different places.

Extra audio

15 - A Birthday Party

Today is my roommate's birthday. We have many things to do before the party. We have to clean the living room and kitchen. Then we have to go shopping. I also have to wrap his present.

It's a new baseball glove. He likes to play baseball. Our friends arrive at 7 o'clock. We eat cake and drink soda. We listen to music and dance. It is a fun party.

16 - I can't Find Rusty

I have to walk my dog, Rusty, after school. We walk to the park. Rusty sees a squirrel. He runs and I can't hold his leash. Rusty chases the squirrel.

I can't catch him. Now I have to find Rusty. I climb a tree so I can see. I look for Rusty. He is near the playground. I run across the field and get him. Now we can go home.

17 - A Messy Room

I have to clean my room. There are dirty clothes on the floor. There are clean clothes on my bed. There are books and trash everywhere. I put the dirty clothes in the laundry basket.

I fold the clean clothes. I put them in the dresser. I put the books on my desk. I put the trash in the trash can. I sweep the floor. Now my room is not messy.

18 - At the Lake

In the summer, my family goes to the lake. There are many cabins at the lake. We rent a small cabin on the shore. I spend a lot of time with my sisters. We are always near the water. There is a dock behind the cabin. We can jump off the dock into the lake. Or we can fish from the dock. There is a small boat we can use. We row out to the island. I like to visit the lake.

19 - A Test Tomorrow

I have a test tomorrow. I like to study with my friend Sarah. She is very smart. We meet at the library. But it is too crowded. There aren't any empty tables. We go to Sarah's house.

But it's too noisy. Her brother is having a piano lesson. Finally, we go to my house. There aren't any people at my house. It is quiet and we can study. I want to get a good grade on the test.

20 - A Snow Day

There is no school today. It is snowing too much. I want to go outside to play. I have to put on a warm hat, a coat, and gloves. My neighbors are playing in the snow. They are building a snowman.

I help finish the snowman. Then we have a snowball fight. Now we are cold so we go inside. We sit by the fireplace and drink hot chocolate. It is still snowing outside.

21 - My Busy Day

I am so busy today. I have a lot of homework. I am writing an essay for my English class. And I am studying for my math test. Then, I have to prepare for an event.

We are having a bake sale at school. We need money for a field trip. I am making signs to post at school. And I am baking cookies to sell. Tonight, I am going to my grandfather's birthday party. So I have to finish everything first. It is rather stressful.

22 - A Science Project

Today is an important day. I am doing a science experiment in class. I need to listen to the directions. So I do not make a mistake.

I am working with a partner. We begin the experiment. We are mixing chemicals together. Then we look at them with a microscope. The chemicals are turning green. We write our results in our notebooks. It is very exciting. Something fun is always happening in my science class. I am not going to be bored here.

23 - The Baseball Game

Mark has tickets to a baseball game. He is going with his friend Paul. Paul's dad is driving them to the stadium. But his dad is really late.

Mark calls Paul's home. Paul has bad news. He isn't coming to pick Mark up. His dad isn't feeling well. Paul's dad has an idea. He gives his ticket to Mark's dad. Mark's dad drives them to the baseball game. They arrive at the stadium on time. They aren't going to miss the start of the game. Paul buys his dad a souvenir.

24 - A Long Weekend

I have an extra day off from school this weekend. My family and I are going to take a train to the city. We have tickets to see a play on Friday.

On Saturday, we are going to spend the day downtown. We are going to visit the museum and go shopping. Then, we are going to go to a nice restaurant Saturday night. On Sunday morning, we are going to walk through the city garden. My mom likes to take pictures of the flowers there. They are very colorful. We will take the train home after brunch. It is going to be a fun weekend.

25 - The Inventor

My friend, Mike, has a lot of crazy ideas. He builds many interesting machines. He likes to do science experiments. He is always thinking of new inventions.

Right now, he is designing a robot. The robot can move up the stairs. It can also carry things. Mike is going to be an inventor. Or maybe he can be a scientist. He wants to create new things. He is going to be famous.

26 - My Favorite Season

Spring is my favorite season. The weather is warm but not too hot. I like spending time outdoors. I go to the park a lot.

Or I hike in the forest. I smell the fresh air. The trees are growing new leaves. Many flowers are blooming. I like photographing the flowers. I also like working in the yard. I plant a vegetable garden and keep the yard nice. Spring is busy but it is also a lot of fun.

27 - A Vacation

My family and I are planning a vacation. We are going to Hawaii for a week. I am very excited for our trip. We are staying next to the beach.

I am going to swim all day. I want to play in the sand and build sand castles. There are other activities to do, too. We can go hiking and see a waterfall. I want to go to a luau. We can eat local food and see hula dancing. It is going to be a great vacation.

28 - At the Beach

I am going to the beach with my older sister. We are leaving at noon. We are taking the bus. We wait at the bus stop at the end of the street.

We finally arrive at the beach. We change into our swimsuits and go swimming. Later, we walk to the lighthouse. There is one at this beach. We climb to the top and see the view. We can see whales in the ocean. Then, we walk back to the beach. My sister spends a lot of time here. She is a lifeguard.

29 - A Winter Trip

My family and I are taking a winter trip. We are going to the mountains for four days. My aunt and uncle are also coming with us. We are staying at a ski resort. We are going skiing and snowboarding. I have some questions before we leave. Are we taking our dog? Is my cousin coming?

I ask my mom for details. She answers my questions. We are not taking our dog because pets are not allowed. My grandfather is going to take care of our dog. My cousin is coming but she can't ski. She has a broken leg. She wants to relax by the fire in the cabin.

30 - A Beautiful Day

It is a beautiful day so I want to be outside. I call my friend Alice. Do you want to play tennis? But she has to look after her baby sister.

I send a message to my friend Jenny. Do you have any free time today? She responds immediately. Do you want to go to the zoo? We meet at the gate at one o'clock. We see tigers, elephants, and monkeys. The penguins are my favorite. I like going to the zoo.
